About JobHunt

One place to find a job, check your resume and see where every application stands.

About our service

Job seekers enter their skills once. We rank every job by how many required skills they already have. Recruiters post the full job details: role, salary, CTC, working hours, WFO/WFH, probation, working days and bond.

What you get

  • Skill-matched job list
  • Resume analyzer with a missing-skills list
  • Resume maker with 3 templates

Designed for clarity

  • Works on phone, tablet and laptop
  • Status and rejection reasons shown on your dashboard
  • No hidden steps between a job and its Apply button

About user experience

Every job card shows the details people usually have to ask about in the interview: bond, probation and hours. Decisions come with a reason so you know what to improve.

About compliance tie-up

We aim to work with verified employers only. Recruiters must provide a company name, official email and contact number. Job posts must state the full pay and bond terms. Partner and verification details will be listed here when finalised.

Privacy Policy

This demo stores the details you enter (name, contact, skills, applications) only in your own browser using localStorage. Nothing is sent to a server. Clearing your browser data removes it.

When a live version runs with a database, we will collect only what is needed to match you with jobs, will not sell your data, and will let you delete your account and resume on request.
